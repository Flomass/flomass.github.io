# Ecological Commitment

I am very concerned by the envrionmental crisis that we are facing. As the rest of the society, research has to evolve to reduce its ecological impact. Here is what I try to do on the topic.

## Ecopolien
To discuss this question and discuss how society should transform, I am member of a research collectif, l'écopolien ("Atelier d'écologie politique francilien). We organize monthly seminars at "la maison de l'île de France", on the cité universitaire campus. It's open to all, feel free to join.
Our website is [here][ecopolien]

## A la fin du monde il fera beau
Together with Gabriel Perez (philosophy teacher), we wrote a book on climate inaction. Our goal was to try to understand why there 
are so few changes inspite of the many alarming signs.
It was published in 2024 by les éditions de l'atelier. The book is [here][livre]
